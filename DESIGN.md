DESIGN.md — Jasa Angkut Slamet
Design direction: Modern Nusantara — Natural, Trustworthy, Human.

Brand personality: Amanah, sederhana, profesional, ramah, dan membumi.

1. Design Philosophy

Jasa Angkut Slamet adalah layanan transportasi dan angkut barang lokal yang melayani Demak, Kota Semarang, dan Kabupaten Semarang.

Website harus memberikan rasa percaya kepada calon pelanggan sejak detik pertama.

Prinsip desain:

Clarity over decoration.
Real photography over AI-looking illustrations.
Usability over unnecessary animation.
Trust over exaggerated marketing.
Mobile-first experience.
Fast access to WhatsApp and price estimation.

Jangan membuat website terlihat seperti template SaaS, startup finansial, atau landing page AI generator.

Accessibility

Provide visible focus states.
Use readable text sizes on mobile. 3. Typography

Recommended fonts:

Headings: Manrope.
Body: Inter.
Fallback: system-ui, sans-serif.

Typography rules:

Use sentence case.
Avoid excessive uppercase headings.
Avoid oversized typography on every section.
Keep paragraph widths comfortable.
Use font weights 400, 500, 600 and 700.

Suggested scale:

Element Size
Hero heading 42–52px desktop
Section heading 28–34px
Card heading 18–20px
Body 15–16px
Caption 13–14px
Mobile hero 32–38px 4. Layout

Container:

Maximum width: 1160px.
Center aligned.
Horizontal padding: 24px desktop.
Horizontal padding: 18px mobile.

Spacing:

Use an 8px spacing system.
Section spacing: 72–96px desktop.
Section spacing: 48–64px mobile.
Card gap: 16–24px.

Border radius:

Buttons: 10px.
Cards: 12px.
Large sections: 16px.
Avoid excessive pill-shaped elements.

Shadows:

Prefer subtle borders.
Use shadows only when needed to establish hierarchy.
Avoid floating glassmorphism panels. 5. Navigation

WhatsApp CTA on right.
Sticky navigation only if it improves usability.

Accessible navigation.

Section 1 — Hero

Headline: "Pindahan lebih tenang. Barang sampai dengan aman."

Description: "Jasa angkut barang dan pindahan untuk rumah, kos, toko, maupun kebutuhan usaha Anda."

Primary CTA: "Pesan via WhatsApp"

Secondary CTA: "Hitung Estimasi Tarif"

Visual: Use actual photographs of Slamet's operational vehicle, loading process, or delivery activities.

Do not use generic AI-generated trucks.

Section 2 — Services

Four service categories:

Pindahan Rumah
Angkut Barang
Pindahan Kos
Angkut Kebutuhan Usaha

Use simple line icons.

Each card must have a concise description.

Section 3 — Price Estimator

This is a primary product feature.

Include:

Pickup location.
Destination location.
Map interaction.
Distance calculation.
Vehicle selection, if supported.
Additional service options, if applicable.
Estimated price.
WhatsApp booking action.

Keep the existing pricing logic intact.

Clearly distinguish estimates from confirmed final prices.

Never fabricate rates or distance calculations.

Section 4 — How It Works
Pilih lokasi penjemputan.
Masukkan lokasi tujuan.
Periksa estimasi tarif.
Konfirmasi melalui WhatsApp.

Use a simple numbered horizontal layout on desktop and vertical layout on mobile.

Section 5 — Why Choose Us

Communicate real, verifiable advantages:

Informasi tarif yang jelas.
Komunikasi langsung.
Area pelayanan yang terdefinisi.
Proses pemesanan sederhana.

Do not invent customer testimonials, ratings, years of experience, or guarantees.

Section 6 — Service Area

Highlight:

Kabupaten Demak.
Kota Semarang.
Kabupaten Semarang.

Use a real map or geographic visualization.

Section 7 — Final CTA

Headline: "Ada barang yang perlu diangkut?"

Description: "Hubungi kami untuk membicarakan kebutuhan angkut dan pindahan Anda."

CTA: "Hubungi Slamet via WhatsApp"

Section 8 — Footer

Include:

Brand.
Service areas.
Operating hours.
WhatsApp contact.
Important navigation links. 7. Components

Buttons:

Primary: Forest green background, white text.
Secondary: White background, forest green border.
Accent: Amber background, dark text.

Cards:

White background.
Thin natural border.
12px radius.
Minimal shadow.

Forms:

Clearly labeled fields.
Helpful validation messages.
Large touch targets.
Visible loading and error states.

Map:

Clean map presentation.
Clear origin and destination markers.
Responsive dimensions.
Accessible fallback when map services fail.

WhatsApp:

Use the existing official business contact.
Preserve existing contact details.
Prefill a useful booking message.
Never hide WhatsApp behind multiple navigation steps. 8. Photography Direction

Photography should feel documentary and authentic.

Preferred:

Actual business vehicle.
Real loading and unloading activities.
Residential Indonesian environments.
Real streets and delivery locations.
Natural daylight.
Real employees and equipment, with permission.

Avoid:

Artificial 3D illustrations.
Generic corporate stock photography.
Unrealistic vehicles.
Overly polished AI-generated scenes.
Decorative images that do not explain the service. 9. Motion & Interaction

Use motion only when it provides feedback.

Recommended:

150–200ms transitions.
Subtle hover states.
Button press feedback.
Smooth but restrained section transitions.
Loading indicators during map calculations.

Avoid:

Parallax everywhere.
Constant floating elements.
Auto-playing carousels.
Excessive scroll animations.
Unnecessary animated gradients.

Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

10. Responsive Behavior

Mobile is the primary design priority.

Requirements:

No horizontal overflow.
Minimum 44px touch targets.
Readable forms.
Accessible map controls.
Prominent booking CTA.
Optimized image loading.
Fixed bottom CTA only when it does not obscure content. 11. Performance
Use optimized WebP or AVIF images.
Lazy-load below-the-fold images.
Avoid excessive dependencies.
Prevent layout shifts.
Keep JavaScript lightweight.
Optimize map loading.
Preserve existing SEO metadata. 12. Anti-AI-Slop Rules

Never:

Use generic gradient hero backgrounds.
Add random decorative blobs.
Put every section inside rounded cards.
Use excessive glassmorphism.
Add meaningless statistics.
Invent testimonials.
Use generic AI illustrations.
Repeat identical three-column card layouts everywhere.
Add unnecessary animations.
Use oversized marketing copy without useful information.

Always:

Use real business context.
Show actual services.
Make pricing understandable.
Prioritize WhatsApp conversion.
Maintain consistent spacing.
Use authentic local photography.
Write natural Indonesian copy.
Preserve useful existing features. 13. Final Design Goal

The final experience should feel like a trustworthy, well-managed Indonesian local transportation business.

Not a generic AI landing page.

Not an overly decorative Islamic template.

A clean, functional, authentic business website built around trust, transparency, and ease of booking.
